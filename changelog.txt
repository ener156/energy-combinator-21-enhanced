Version: 1.3.0
Date: 17.11.2026
  Features:
    - New Overview, Settings and Diagnostics interface with scaling, rounding
      and electric-network measurements.
    - Three MK2 measuring modes: automatic circuit filters, entire grid, and
      saved manual building filters with optional quality selection.
    - New circuit signals for stored accumulator energy and total storage
      capacity, using the selected scaling factor.
    - Improved support for quality-aware energy statistics and space platforms.
  Changes:
    - Only one player may edit a combinator's settings at a time; other
      players can still view live measurements. The original combinator
      interface shares the same edit lock.
    - Recommend 7 Segment Digits as an optional companion mod; not required.
  Bugfixes:
    - Prevent incorrect power spikes after changing MK2 filters or reconnecting
      electric networks; update measurements for enabled buildings.
    - Improve power limits, signal rounding and storage calculations.
    - Improve GUI layout and error messages for multiplayer users.
