Version: 1.3.0
Date: 17.11.2026
  Features:
    - Added Overview, Settings and Diagnostics tabs with live power measurements.
    - Expanded MK2 with automatic circuit filters, full-network mode, and
      saved manual building filters with optional quality selection.
    - Added circuit signals for stored accumulator energy and total storage
      capacity, measured in joules.
  Changes:
    - Added a shared multiplayer edit lock for the enhanced settings and the
      original combinator interface. Other players can still view measurements.
    - Added 7 Segment Digits as an optional recommended companion mod.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed GUI opening on space platforms and improved multiplayer GUI handling.
    - Corrected power estimates for quality-aware buildings and accumulators.
    - Improved rounding, filter switching and electric-network change handling.

Version: 1.2.1
Date: 08.11.2026
  Bugfixes:
