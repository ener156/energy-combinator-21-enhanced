Version: 1.2.6
Date: 10.11.2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Avoid losing one full signal point of estimated maximum production at exact scaling boundaries
      due to floating-point precision (e.g. 800 kW at factor X=5 now reports 8, not 7).
    - Only normalize values indistinguishably close to an integer; genuinely fractional
      maximum production values continue rounding down.
  Compatibility:
    - Current power measurements and maximum consumption rounding are unchanged.
    - The user-selected scaling factor, default 5-tick interval and Tier 2 filters are unchanged.

Version: 1.2.5
Date: 08.11.2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Round estimated maximum production downward after applying scaling, so the circuit signal never overstates the estimated production capacity.
    - Keep maximum consumption rounded upward and current measurements rounded to the nearest integer.
  Improvements:
    - Tier 1 combinators on ordinary local electric networks now read current consumption and production from the last electric network tick.
    - Current power signals follow load changes more accurately, reducing lag after consumers are added or removed.
  Compatibility:
    - The maximum-consumption estimate, Tier 2 entity filters and experimental global electric networks retain their existing calculations.
    - Space-platform and multiplayer compatibility remain experimental.
    - The configured output update interval is unchanged.
