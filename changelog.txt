Version: 1.2.0
Date: 07.11.2026
  Changes:
    - Released Energy Combinator 2.1 Enhanced as an independent, community-maintained fork with its own mod ID.
    - Updated compatibility for Factorio 2.1 while preserving the original in-game entity, recipe and signal prototype names.
    - Added experimental support for global electric network statistics.
    - Included explicit incompatibilities with the original Energy Combinator and other overlapping forks.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed the Factorio 2.1 read-only entity.minable error by using minable_flag instead.
    - Corrected on_object_destroyed registration lookups and cleanup of hidden helper entities.
    - Added guards for missing filter slots, missing signals, hidden entities and unknown entity prototypes.
    - Corrected signed 32-bit signal bounds and handling of the last circuit filter slot.
    - Improved tracking and cleanup of object destruction registrations.
  Optimizations:
    - Avoided unnecessary writes to lamp circuit conditions when their values have not changed.
  Compatibility:
    - Incorporates the Factorio 2.1 compatibility update also documented by n0t49a1n in the separate Energy Combinator 2.1 fork version 2.1.1 (24.10.2026).
    - Incorporates the minable read-only error fix also documented by n0t49a1n in the separate fork version 2.1.2 (24.10.2026).
    - Versions 2.1.1 and 2.1.2 were releases of that other fork, not earlier releases of Energy Combinator 2.1 Enhanced.
  Info:
    - Factorio 2.1 startup and 120-tick isolated runtime smoke tests passed. Full gameplay, multiplayer, save migration and space-platform tests remain outstanding.
    - Original mod: Energy Combinator by VinDust; based on Power Combinator by dodo.the.last. License: The Unlicense.
