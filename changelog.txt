Version: 1.3.0
Date: 17.11.2026
  Features:
    - Added a new Overview, Settings and Diagnostics interface with live power
      measurements, configurable rounding and detailed network information.
    - Expanded MK2 with automatic circuit filters, full-network mode and
      saved manual building filters, including optional quality selection.
    - Added circuit signals for stored accumulator energy and total storage
      capacity, expressed in joules.
  Changes:
    - Added a shared multiplayer editing lock for the enhanced settings and
      original combinator interface. Other players can still view measurements.
    - Added 7 Segment Digits as an optional recommended companion mod.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed GUI opening on Space Age platforms and improved multiplayer
      interface handling.
    - Improved power calculations for quality-aware buildings and accumulators.
    - Fixed rounding edge cases, MK2 filter-switch spikes and electric-network
      transition handling.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.2.5
Date: 08.11.2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Round estimated maximum production downward after applying scaling, so the circuit signal never overstates the estimated production capacity.
    - Keep maximum consumption rounded upward and current measurements rounded to the nearest integer.
  Improvements:
    - Tier 1 combinators on ordinary local electric networks now read current consumption and production from the last electric network tick.
    - Current power signals follow load changes more accurately, reducing lag after consumers are added or removed.
  Compatibility:
    - The maximum-consumption estimate, Tier 2 entity filters and experimental global electric networks retain their existing calculations.
    - Space-platform and multiplayer compatibility remain experimental.
    - The configured output update interval is unchanged.
